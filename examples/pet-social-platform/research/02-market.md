Method note: S01 (white-paper excerpt PDF) and S05 (iResearch white paper) were read first-hand — S05 is an

**Ownership (feeds `marketKpis[0]`)**
- 76.89M urban dog & cat owners in 2024, +2.4% YoY (dogs 36.01M, cats 40.88M); series 2018: 56.48M → 2023: 75.10M. (S01, first-hand PDF; urban, dogs & cats only, exotic pets excluded.)
- Urban dog & cat population 124.11M in 2024 (S01); 126M in 2025 — dogs 53.43M +1.6%, cats 72.89M +1.9% (S02, CCTV reprint of the 2026 edition launch).
- The white paper does **not** publish a household-penetration % — the plan's original question is unanswerable in white-paper terms. Nearest: iResearch, 17.8% dog / 16.4% cat household penetration, 2023, national household base (S06, secondary). A widely circulated "22% of households, 2023" has no named originating institution and was not used.
- City-tier split of urban owners, 2024: tier-1 32.5% (+3.6pp), tier-2 42.5%, tier-3 & below 25.0% (S01) — supports the case's tier-1/2 focus (75% combined).

**Content consumption (feeds `marketKpis[1]`)**
- No public survey measures "x% of owners consume pet content weekly". Platform-side figures are all all-user reach, not owner-share.
- 53% of core pet owners get pet information on Xiaohongshu; 63% of new pet owners share pet content on Xiaohongshu weekly (S04, CBNData — co-publisher release page, 2024).
- Douyin pet content: 300M+ users reached monthly, 150M+ pet-interest users, 2024 (S07, secondary reprint of Ocean Engine material); 100M pet-interest users at 2023Q1 (same series, earlier point —口径 may differ, not connected as a trend).
- Xiaohongshu pet-interest population 170M at 2025-06, doubled vs 2024-03; official claim that pet-owner users open the app 25–30×/day (S08, secondary reprint of an Asia Pet Fair release).
- Weibo pet-interest users 130M+ at 2021-09; Kuaishou: 290M 18+ users had watched pet content by 2021-08 (S16, S17 — dated, context only).

**Social interaction (feeds `marketKpis[2]`)**
- No survey quantifies general online owner-to-owner interaction. The original "41% have interacted with another owner online" cannot be sourced as stated.
- Closest, adopted for the KPI: 22.5% of surveyed owners interact in online pet communities to exchange illness/care experience — iResearch survey 2022-12, diagnosis sub-sample N=1211, TGI 133 for ages 21–25 (S05, p.40, first-hand).
- Related: 55.6% discuss pet-health issues with owners around them (offline, S05 p.21); 20%+ of owners consult pet friends for disease information (S09, 2026 white-paper launch via 光明网); 63% of Xiaohongshu new owners share weekly (S04, platform-internal sample — would overstate the general population, used as note context only).

**Local service use (feeds `marketKpis[3]`)**
- No authoritative "visits per pet per quarter" exists anywhere found. The original 3.4×/quarter placeholder was replaced by a penetration metric.
- Grooming consumption penetration 51.4% of urban owners, 2024 (down 2.4pp vs 2023); dogs 63.3%, cats 31.9% (S01, first-hand).
- Frequency distributions that do exist: 26.5% of owners do a pet check-up every 6 months, 24.9% yearly, 24.2% every 2–3 months, 8.4% monthly (S05 p.34, N=957; 86.5% do regular check-ups, p.3). 41.08% of polled owners take the pet to a vet clinic 3–4×/year (S10, media poll 2022, n=1060 — weaker, note only).
- Channel/decision context: 89.8% use offline vet hospitals, 58.2% have used online vet consultations (S05 p.40); "distance from home" is the top factor in choosing pet care, 57.9% (S11, Meituan 2023); Meituan pet-service merchants 30k+, orders +24% H1 2025 (S12, secondary).
- 2026 edition service datapoints: grooming done at pet stores by 78.4% of owners; boarding at pet stores 48.5%; in-clinic treatment dogs 52.0% / cats 39.6%; 36.5% deworm 2–3×/year (S02/S09).

**Spending & category split (feeds `marketKpis[4]`, `spendTrend`)**
- Per-pet annual spend: dog ¥2,961 (+3.0%), cat ¥2,020 (+4.9%), 2024; dog series 2021–24: 2634/2882/2875/2961, cat 1826/1883/1870/2020 (S01, first-hand). 2025: dog ¥3,006 (+1.5%), cat ¥2,085 (+3.2%) (S02, secondary).
- The original ¥4.8k/pet placeholder was ~60% above the sourced dog figure and ~2.4× the cat figure.
- Urban dog & cat market: 2021–2025: ¥249.0/270.6/279.3/300.2/312.6bn (S01, S02). Keep this apart from wider scopes: iResearch ¥345.3bn "national pet market" 2024 (S06); Statista/KPMG ¥592.8bn "pet economy" 2023 (S14). Three different pots — never mix.
- Category shares of urban spend: 2022 food 50.7% / medical 29.1% / supplies 13.3% / services 6.8% (S03); 2024: 52.8% / 28.0% / 12.4% / 6.8% (S01); 2025: 53.7% / 27.6% / 12.2% / 6.5% (S02). Earlier: food 61.4% / medical 19.0% (2019), medical 29.2% (2021) (S15, broker reprint of earlier editions).
- `spendTrend` was rebuilt as share × market size, indexed 2022 = 100 (2023 omitted; its four-way split is not public): food 100→116→122, services 100→111→110, health 100→107→110. Derivation in the provenance note.
- Growth-rate sources found only forecasts by category with mismatched windows (food CAGR 14.9% 2023–28E, pharma 10.4% 2025–29E — S14); no source gives a "services" CAGR. Not used in data.

**Need layers (feeds `needLayers` — qualitative, no data change)**
- Functional: medical is the #2 spend category (28.0%, S01); grooming penetration >50% (S01); pet-medical institutions 34,426 in 2024, +18.5% (unverified aggregator figure — not used beyond this note).
- Emotional: 49.9% treat the pet as family + 23.7% as their own child (S05 p.19, N=2067, 2022 — adopted over the older 59.1% "as child" from the 2019 edition, S18); motives: companionship/less loneliness 62.0% (TGI 124 for people living alone), fun 64.2%, stress relief 56.7% (S05); pet funerals and pet photography exist as measured penetration categories in S01 — emotional demand commercialised.
- Social: qualitative only. ~20 offline pet events in Shanghai in two weeks, 2023-12 (S13); "pet-friendly" searches +80%, notes +224% on Meituan/Dianping, 2025 (S12); walk-buddy phenomenon covered by press. **No serious sampled survey quantifies pet-social participation** — the layer stands on qualitative evidence, as the page's judgement fields say.

**Value matrix (`valueMatrix`) — stays hypothesis**
- No survey ranks pet surfaces by functional × emotional value. Spend structure (food > medical > supplies > services, S01) and the "distance first" decision factor (S11) are consistent with services clustering on the functional axis, but positions remain judgement.

## Evidence for / against the chapter's argument

- **Against "spend is shifting toward services / services grow fastest"** (S01, S02, S03, S15): services' share of urban pet spend is flat at 6.5–6.8% since 2022; food's share is rising; the structural riser was medical (19.0% in 2019 → 29.2% in 2021, ~28% plateau since). → Revised; row added to `research/revisions.md`. The chapter's argument survives in adjusted form: the shift is goods → care (medical), and services growth is visible in penetration (pet travel +4.9pp, insurance 18.9%, S02) rather than spend share.
- **For the emotional/social framing**: 73.7% treat the pet as family or child (S05); companionship is the #2 motive and strongest among people living alone (S05); offline pet-social events and "pet-friendly" demand growing (S12, S13).
- **For the decision-layer thesis**: pet-care choices are trust- and proximity-driven (57.9% "close to home", S11); disease information flows through vets and peers (69.1% ask a vet, 20%+ ask pet friends, S09).
- Counter-evidence was actively looked for on every KPI; the one conflict found is the services-growth claim above.
